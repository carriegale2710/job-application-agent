# Case study template

A resume shows what you did. A case study shows how you thought and why you made each decision. Aim for a 3-5 minute read.

Adapted from `portfolio-case-study-writer` in [ResumeSkills](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Resume Skills.

## Structure

```markdown
# <Project name>

## Quick facts
- **Role:** <your role, e.g. "Front-end developer (1 of 3)">
- **Organisation:** <client, employer, course or personal>
- **Timeline:** <duration, Mon YYYY>
- **Team:** <who else, by role>
- **Tools:** <stack or methods>
- **Result:** <one line, with a number if there is one>

## Problem
<The context, who had the problem, and why it mattered. 2-4 sentences.>

## Process
### Research
<What you found out, and how: interviews, analytics, audits, reading code>
### Approach
<How you tackled it>
### Key decisions
<2-3 choices you made, the options you considered, and why you chose this one>

## Solution
<What you delivered. Describe each key screen, feature or component, and point to its image.>

## Results
| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|

## What I learned
<What worked, and what you would do differently>

## Images needed
- <before and after screens, diagrams, code snippets, results charts>
```

## Emphasis by role

| Role | Focus on |
|---|---|
| UX or product designer | Research, design process, iterations, usability results |
| Front-end developer | UI decisions, accessibility, performance, component design, links to the live site and repo |
| Software engineer | Architecture, trade-offs, debugging, testing, performance |
| Product manager or business analyst | Prioritisation, stakeholders, metrics, the decisions and their reasons |

## Checklist

- [ ] Your part is clear, separate from the team's
- [ ] Every fact and number is in `resume/master-resume.md`
- [ ] Results are quantified, or the case study says why they could not be
- [ ] Key decisions show reasons, not just the steps
- [ ] Confidential data is blurred, and the user has permission to show client work
- [ ] Every image has a caption
- [ ] The user can talk through every part at interview
